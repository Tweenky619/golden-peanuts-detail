Ceramic Coating & Pricing

Long-lasting protection and a deep, glossy shine that keeps your paint looking newer, longer.

Why Ceramic Coating?

A ceramic coating bonds to your paint to give it a tougher, glossier, easier-to-clean finish than wax alone.

💧

Hydrophobic Shine

Water beads and rolls right off, taking dirt and grime with it.

☀️

UV & Oxidation Protection

Helps guard your paint against fading and oxidation from the San Diego sun.

🧽

Easier Washing

Dirt and grime have a harder time sticking, so future washes are quicker.

⏳

Long-Lasting Protection

Outlasts traditional wax, keeping that just-detailed look for longer.

What's Included

Every ceramic coating service follows the same careful process, whether it's an add-on or standalone.

1

Wash & Decontamination

A full hand wash plus iron decontamination and clay bar to strip away old wax, tar, and bonded contaminants.

2

Paint Prep

A light polish pass to prep the surface so the coating bonds directly and evenly to clean paint.

3

Ceramic Application

The ceramic coating is hand-applied across all exterior panels for even, complete coverage.

4

Cure & Inspection

Proper cure time followed by a final inspection and wipe-down for a streak-free, even finish.
