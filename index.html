Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really come to me?

Yes — we're a fully mobile detailing service. We come to your home, office, or wherever your vehicle is parked, anywhere in the San Diego area.

What vehicles do you work on?

Cars, SUVs, trucks, boats, trailers, RVs, and planes. If it needs a detail, we can likely handle it — just ask.

How do I pay?

We accept Venmo and Zelle. Payment is due when the job is complete.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Golden Peanut's Detail is licensed and insured, with over 10 years of detailing experience.

Do you speak Spanish?

Sí — we speak both English and Spanish. ¡Hablamos español!

What if my vehicle needs extra attention?

Excessive mess (heavy hair, sand, mud, stains, odors, and similar) may incur an extra charge of $30 per 30 additional minutes required. We'll always let you know before starting extra work.
